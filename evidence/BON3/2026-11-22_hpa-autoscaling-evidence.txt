[18:56:37] ANALYSIS: the HPA works mechanically (it computes a desired size from CPU as a percentage of request: 354% of 100m against the 70% target and is limited to the maximum of 5). But BON-3 asks for capacity that goes up AND down with load. Here the aggregator's baseline CPU while idle (about 350m per replica against a 100m request) is far above the target, so the HPA stays at 5 replicas regardless of load, and scale-down was NOT observed. The scale-up events from earlier in the day (rescale 3 -> 5) are no longer in the event list (events expire after one hour).
[18:56:37] CONSEQUENCE: BON-3 is NOT demonstrated on k3s. To demonstrate it the idle CPU of the aggregator must fall below the target (see the idle fan-out finding and the CPU profiles) or the target/request must be re-tuned, then run load up and down while recording kubectl get hpa -w.
